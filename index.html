Process

From VOD to TikTok in Four Steps

ClipStream automates the entire clip-to-post pipeline so you can focus on streaming, not editing.

1

Connect Twitch

Authorize ClipStream with your Twitch account. We scan your channel for top-performing clips using Twitch's Clips API.

2

Auto-Discover Top Clips

Our algorithm ranks your clips by view count, engagement, and recency to surface your best moments automatically.

3

Format as 9:16 Vertical Video

Clips are intelligently reframed and reformatted for TikTok's vertical 9:16 format with smart cropping that keeps the action in frame.

4

Generate Captions & Post

AI generates platform-optimized captions and hashtags, then posts directly to TikTok via the official Content Posting API.
